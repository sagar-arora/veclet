# Code of Conduct

Veclet contributors, reviewers, and maintainers are expected to make project
spaces respectful, constructive, and safe for participation.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction professionally.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and project security practices.
- Stop behavior when a maintainer identifies it as disruptive or harmful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an
available private GitHub contact or moderation channel. Maintainers will limit
disclosure to what is necessary to investigate and act. Responses may include a
warning, temporary participation restriction, removal of contributions, or a
permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

Maintainers who are directly involved in a report must recuse themselves when
another qualified maintainer is available.
